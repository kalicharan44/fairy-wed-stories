## Scope and method

The original JP Events checkout was read-only. Its legacy content contained Pexels CDN URLs and Postimage URLs whose filenames referenced Pexels photo IDs. Selected IDs were retrieved from their canonical Pexels CDN URLs and saved locally in the Fairy Wed Stories project. This verifies a source match, not JP Events ownership or Fairy Wed Stories association.

Pexels permits website use without attribution. That does not establish model, property, venue, event, photographer-assignment, or endorsement rights. Those details are unverified. The original files listed below remain archived and excluded from production output. Two selected photos were fetched again from Pexels at 3840 px wide and are used in the website as illustrative inspiration under neutral filenames; they are not represented as Fairy or JP Events client work.
